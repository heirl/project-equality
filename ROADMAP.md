# Project Equality — Roadmap

This roadmap prioritizes low-cost, phone-friendly work and evidence over grand claims.

## Phase 1 — Establish the foundation

- Publish the founding principle and draft constitution.
- Invite constructive criticism and identify unclear terms.
- Keep a public change log for important revisions.
- Avoid collecting personal or sensitive information.

## Phase 2 — Improve the impact-review checklist

- Test the questions against realistic, low-risk examples.
- Identify missing stakeholder, accessibility, privacy, and security considerations.
- Make every warning understandable and actionable.
- Clearly distinguish self-reported answers from independently verified evidence.

## Phase 3 — Community review

- Invite people with different backgrounds and areas of expertise.
- Record disagreements respectfully.
- Seek feedback from people likely to be affected by proposed systems.
- Document conflicts of interest and limitations.

## Phase 4 — Evaluation and learning

- Define measurable outcomes for specific use cases.
- Compare proposed safeguards with observed outcomes.
- Review failures and update the framework.
- Seek independent evaluation before making claims of effectiveness.

## Non-goals and safeguards

The project will not claim to certify AI systems, prove fairness, detect consciousness, or replace legal compliance, security reviews, audits, or democratic governance. A checklist can help people ask better questions; it cannot guarantee safe or ethical outcomes.

## Working principle

Start small. Be transparent about uncertainty. Prefer testable improvements over unsupported promises.
