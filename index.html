AI Application Human Impact Assessment
Explore how an AI application could benefit or harm people before and after deployment. This framework applies to all AI applications; higher-impact uses need deeper scrutiny.

Choose assessment mode

Start with a quick automated screening from your idea, or complete the detailed manual assessment below. You can use both.

Automated screening uses transparent, locally run rules and keyword patterns. It is an initial triage aid—not a generative AI expert, factual verification, legal review, or final risk decision. It may miss context and should be followed by manual assessment.

1. Describe the AI application

2. Potential benefits

Select plausible benefits; add evidence rather than assuming they will occur.

3. Potential harms and risk areas

Select every area that could plausibly arise. Selection means “investigate this”, not “harm is proven”.

4. Scale, severity, and exposure

5. Human control and safeguards

6. Lifecycle and evidence

The report separates selected concerns from verified findings. Empty or unknown answers are evidence gaps, not proof of safety.

Important limitations

This is a structured self-assessment aid. It does not inspect models, datasets, source code, security controls, or deployment conditions; independently verify answers; calculate a scientifically validated risk score; or provide legal advice, audit, certification, or a guarantee. It cannot replace domain experts, affected-community consultation, independent evaluation, applicable law, or ongoing human accountability. A high-impact or critical-risk use needs deeper, context-specific assessment before deployment and throughout its lifecycle. Do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information.
